A research lab for Hyperliquid strategies, graded before they are trusted

Every strategy idea is pre-registered in an append-only ledger, with its rule, data range and statistical test fixed before any run. It is then graded by a two-level gate (per window, then pooled with a multiple-comparison correction), and only a pass may spend a single-use holdout. Rules and thresholds are never tuned after the fact, and there are no parameter sweeps. The north star is wealth measured in BTC, not dollars: a strategy is judged against simply holding BTC.
