Strategy loop — batch 2026-45

This week the loop tested 25 trading ideas, each with one fixed set of settings written down before it ran, on market data from May 2023 to June 2025. 2 of them did well enough, after allowing for the luck that comes from testing many ideas at once, to earn a second test on a later year of data (July 2025 to June 2026) that the first test never looked at. No idea passed the second test, so nothing new goes forward to paper trading. Ideas that only trade one direction are judged in bitcoin terms, against simply holding bitcoin, which rose strongly over this period; ideas that are hedged are judged in dollars against holding cash. A failed idea is a normal outcome here: the point of the loop is to reject ideas cheaply and honestly.

Note on directional ideas: they are judged in BTC, against simply holding BTC (the lab's north star). Over this data BTC rose about 4×, so any time out of the market costs a lot of BTC: a directional idea that sat in cash shows a large BTC-denominated loss even if it lost few dollars. That is intended.

Correction 2026-11-05 17:13 UTC: ruin rule (level 1, five ideas)

Ruin rule (2026-11-05): equity cannot go below zero. The carry engine used to let a coin's $2,000 sleeve keep paying fees, slippage and the stress haircut on its fixed $1,000 legs after its equity was gone; the daily returns across zero then compounded below −100 %. Now a sleeve whose equity (book + P&L) reaches ≤ 0 is ruined: its positions are written off at the mark, it never trades again, its return that day is −100 % and it leaves the pooled mean; when every sleeve is ruined the idea's return is −100 % (backtest/multi_pair_engine.py step 6, lab/carry.py, tests/test_ruin.py). Round trips no longer count trades made after a sleeve's ruin.
