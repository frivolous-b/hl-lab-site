Spot l2Book slippage

Slippage = |volume-weighted average fill price / mid − 1| in bps for a taker order of the given notional, walking the asks (buy) or the bids (sell); it includes half the spread; thin = the 20 visible levels do not hold that notional. Same method as the perp probe (results/l2book_slippage_2026-09-28.md). Perp max leverage (Info meta, today)

HL maintenance margin = 1 / (2 × max leverage). A 1× isolated short with margin = entry notional is liquidated when the mark reaches entry fill × 2 / (1 + maintenance margin).
