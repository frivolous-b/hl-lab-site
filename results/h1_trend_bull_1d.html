Caveats (facts about the inputs; the spec is not revisited here)

  • Cost model on 1d bars. CandleEstimatedCostModel prices slippage as 30 % of the mean intra-bar range plus a volume-impact term. On daily bars that is BTC 110 bps, ETH 146 bps, SOL 197 bps per fill, against a 4.5 bps fee — the same registered model the 1h/4h cells used, but it is range-scaled, so on 1d it is by far the largest cost line. Every number above includes it.
  • Pre-mainnet candles. HL's candleSnapshot returns 1d bars from 2022-01-01 with zero volume until 2023-02-26 (SOL 2023-03-04), i.e. before the venue traded; they behave like index history (the impact term is 0 there, the spread proxy still applies).
  • Funding gap. fundingHistory starts 2023-05-12; round trips closed before it carry no funding (BTC 3, ETH 4, SOL 0). Missing hourly settlements are counted, never zero-filled.
  • Single-trade concentration. SOL's PF and mean return come from one trade (window 2023-10-12 → 2024-01-10); its median per-trade return is negative. BTC and ETH win rates are 23-30 %, as expected for a trend rule.
  • Trade count. 12-23 round trips per coin over ~3.7 pre-holdout years, 0-4 per 90-day window: below MIN_TRADES_PER_WINDOW (5) in every window and below N_MIN_POOLED (130) pooled. That is what the INSUFFICIENT_DATA verdicts state.
